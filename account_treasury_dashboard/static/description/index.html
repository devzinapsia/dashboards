Treasury Dashboard

Cash position, reconciliation, investments and a weekly cash projection, straight from the accounting

One screen for the treasury team: banks with their book balance, in-transit items and last statement (the same figures as Odoo's bank reconciliation report), cash, petty cash funds, prepaid and credit cards, foreign currency valued at the rate of the day, unreconciled statement lines by age, investments, a 4 to 8 week cash projection with alerts on negative balances, and the inflows, outflows and internal transfers of the month. Everything is configured per company, and every figure opens its detail. Built on top of the "Dashboards" app.
